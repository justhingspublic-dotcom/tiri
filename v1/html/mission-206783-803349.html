5th TIRI Awards

Calling on investment professionals and financial media to cast your votes for Taiwan's most outstanding IR performers.

Voting

Officially Launched

Voting period: now through August 19, 2026

To elevate the quality of information disclosure and enhance investor relations (IR) practices in Taiwan's capital market, the Taiwan Investor Relations Institute (TIRI) is proud to announce the official launch of the 5th TIRI Awards (2026).

Voting is now open and will continue through August 19, 2026. We warmly encourage institutional investors and financial media professionals to participate.

Eligible Participants
Fund managers and analysts covering Taiwan-listed equities; financial journalists reporting on Taiwan's industries and companies.
How It Works
Each eligible individual may vote independently. There is no limit on the number of participants per organization.
