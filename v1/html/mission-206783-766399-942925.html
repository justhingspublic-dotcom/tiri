TIRI Progress Achievement Award

Recognizing small and mid-sized companies demonstrating strong potential and proactive commitment to IR best practices.

First Edition

1st TIRI Progress
Achievement Award

Evaluation process →

To encourage continuous development and steady advancement in investor relations (IR) among small and mid-sized enterprises, the Taiwan Investor Relations Institute (TIRI) has established the TIRI Progress Achievement Award. This special recognition is designed for companies with relatively smaller market capitalizations and limited resources, yet demonstrating strong potential and a proactive commitment to IR best practices.

Unlike the main TIRI Awards, this award does not involve a public voting process. Instead, winners are selected based on objective data. Eligible candidates include TWSE-listed companies with a market capitalization below NT$30 billion, and TPEx-listed companies with a market capitalization below NT$10 billion.

Their market performance and growth trends are evaluated over a specified period, and the most representative companies demonstrating significant progress are selected. Final recognition is subject to approval by the TIRI Executive Committee.

Through this award, TIRI aims to inspire small and mid-sized companies to strengthen information disclosure and shareholder communication mechanisms, thereby advancing the overall standard of IR practices in Taiwan's capital market and injecting new momentum and innovative value into the ecosystem.
